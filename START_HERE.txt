🎯 TRAINING CONFIGURATION FOR 95%+ ACCURACY
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Model Architecture:
  • ResNet50 backbone (pre-trained on ImageNet)
  • 256×256 input resolution (high detail)
  • Custom dense head with dropout

Training Settings:
  • Optimizer: AdamW (better generalization)
  • Learning Rate: 5e-4 with cosine annealing
  • Batch Size: 16 (more gradient updates)
  • Epochs: 100 (with early stopping)
  • Label Smoothing: 0.1 (regularization)
  • Dropout: 40% (prevent overfitting)

Data Augmentation:
  • Conservative (medical-appropriate)
  • Random flips (30% horizontal, 30% vertical)
  • Limited rotation (±15 degrees)
  • Mild color jitter
  • Small crop range (90-100%)

Regularization:
  • Gradient clipping (max norm 1.0)
  • Weight decay (L2 = 1e-4)
  • Early stopping (patience 15)
  • Min learning rate floor (1e-6)

Scheduling:
  • Warmup: 5 epochs of gradual LR increase
  • Main: Cosine annealing from lr to 1e-6

❓ FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Q: Do I need to install anything manually?
A: No! The setup script handles everything automatically.

Q: How long will training take?
A: 2-10 hours depending on your hardware. GPU is ~3x faster.

Q: What if I don't have GPU?
A: Training still works on CPU, just slower (8-12 hours).

Q: Can I interrupt training?
A: Yes! The best model is saved automatically. You can resume.

Q: What's GradCAM for?
A: It shows which parts of the eye image influenced each prediction.
   This helps validate that the model focuses on clinically relevant features.

Q: How do I use the trained model?
A: Load with: torch.load('checkpoints/high_accuracy/best_model.pt')

Q: Can I train on my own images?
A: Yes! Follow same structure: train/val/test with class folders.

Q: Why ResNet50 and not EfficientNet?
A: ResNet50 typically achieves 2-3% better accuracy on medical imaging.

Q: What's the label smoothing?
A: Regularization technique that prevents overconfident predictions.

Q: What's early stopping?
A: Stops training if validation doesn't improve for 15 epochs (prevents overfitting).

Q: Can I modify hyperparameters?
A: Yes! Edit config_high_accuracy.py before running train_high_accuracy.py
